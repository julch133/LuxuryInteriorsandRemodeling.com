Frequently Asked Questions

Can lighting be updated during a remodel?

Yes. Bathroom lights, kitchen lights, ceiling fans, and other fixtures are often updated during remodeling work.

When is a licensed electrician needed?

Panel work, new circuits, major rewiring, and complicated electrical issues should be handled by a licensed electrician.

Does lighting affect the finished look of a remodel?

Yes. Bad lighting can make new tile, paint, flooring, and cabinets look worse than they are.
