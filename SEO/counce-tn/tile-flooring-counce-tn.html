Frequently Asked Questions

What flooring works well for bathrooms?

Tile and water-resistant flooring options are common bathroom choices, but the right answer depends on the subfloor, moisture exposure, and budget.

Can flooring be installed over old flooring?

Sometimes, but not always. The existing floor has to be stable, level enough, and appropriate for the new material.

Why do transitions matter?

Bad transitions create trip points and make the whole job look unfinished. Clean transitions are part of a professional-looking floor.
