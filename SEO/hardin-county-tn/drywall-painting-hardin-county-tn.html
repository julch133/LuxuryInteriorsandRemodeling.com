Frequently Asked Questions

Can you paint after drywall repair?

Yes. Drywall repair and painting are commonly handled together so the final wall looks consistent.

Can old texture be matched perfectly?

Texture can usually be blended closely, but older walls and previous repairs may not be perfectly identical under certain lighting.

Why does paint look bad even when the color is nice?

Usually because the prep was poor. Rough patches, dirty walls, bad edges, and damaged trim can ruin a good paint color.
