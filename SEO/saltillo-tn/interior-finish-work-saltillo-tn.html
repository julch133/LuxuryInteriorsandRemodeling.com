The details people notice

Finish work after bigger projects

Flooring, bathrooms, kitchens, and drywall repairs often leave behind missing trim, rough edges, exposed gaps, or small unfinished spots. Interior finish work cleans that up so the room feels complete.

Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as interior finish work?

Trim, baseboards, shelves, doors, caulking, hardware, paint touch-ups, and final detail work all fall into this category.

Can finish work be done after flooring?

Yes. Baseboards, quarter round, and transitions are often handled after flooring installation.

Why is finish work important for remodeling?

Because small unfinished edges make the whole project look cheaper, even when the major work was done well.
