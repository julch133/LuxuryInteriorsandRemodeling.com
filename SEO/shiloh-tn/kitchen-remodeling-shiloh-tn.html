Frequently Asked Questions

Do you have to replace cabinets to improve a kitchen?

No. Some kitchens can be improved with paint, hardware, trim, backsplash, flooring, lighting, or targeted cabinet adjustments.

What should be planned first in a kitchen remodel?

Start with layout, appliance locations, plumbing needs, electrical needs, and flooring transitions before choosing finishes.

Can kitchen remodeling be done in stages?

Yes. Many homeowners phase the work by starting with flooring, paint, backsplash, fixtures, or cabinets depending on budget and urgency.
