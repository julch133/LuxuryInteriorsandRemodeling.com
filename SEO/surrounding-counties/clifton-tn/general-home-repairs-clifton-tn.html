Frequently Asked Questions

What kinds of small repairs can be grouped together?

Drywall patches, trim, doors, shelves, caulking, fixtures, and paint touch-ups can often be handled as one punch-list project.

Are small home repairs worth doing before selling?

Yes. Small visible issues can make buyers assume the home has been poorly maintained.

Can repairs be added to a remodeling project?

Yes. Remodeling is often the best time to fix nearby damage, trim, drywall, flooring, and fixture issues.
